Jewellery Care

  • Avoid water, perfume, cosmetics, sweat and harsh chemicals.
  • Wipe jewellery gently with a soft dry cloth after use.
  • Store pieces separately in a dry pouch or box to reduce scratching and tarnishing.
  • Remove jewellery before bathing, swimming, sleeping or exercising.
